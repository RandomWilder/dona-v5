## What was proved

- **Mapping is a row; the CHECK on `target` is the migration.** Allowed this slice:
  `tenancy.start_date`, `tenancy.end_date`. Seed writes three mappings (`lease.start_date`,
  `lease.end_date`, `lease_amendment.new_end_date`). No `promotes_to` on E15/E16.
- **Stamp is on `extracted_field`.** Raw `UPDATE`/`INSERT` of `promoted_*` without
  `dona.promoting = on` is **`23001`**. A stamped row cannot be deleted. Register
  `upsertTenancy` still writes dates — those columns are not locked.
- **Unmapped fields cannot become truth.** `apartment_number` promote is `invalid`; the tenancy
  `start_date` does not move. The read page labels them `נקרא בלבד` and offers no promote control.
- **TenancyEvent pulled forward**, `kind = 'amended'` only, source document required, append-only
  (`23001` on UPDATE/DELETE). Clock-driven kinds remain week 5's. `upsertTenancy` does not write
  events.
- **Re-extract deletes unstamped rows only.** A promoted `start_date` survives a second extract.
- **R9.** `src/policy/`, `src/scope/`, `src/calls/` contain no `extracted_field`. Isolation still
  reads typed columns.
