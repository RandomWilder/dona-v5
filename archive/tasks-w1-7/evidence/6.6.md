## Decided here

**Wholesale, not word by word** — `312`, `345`, `678` as three OCR tokens match no per-token pattern.
**On every type, not the types that declare the field** — a declaration governs what is captured, not
what a page prints. **`IDENTIFIER_RUN` is boundary-anchored**, and its own test is the argument:
2,000 UUIDs, 2,000 sha256 digests, 200 base64 page images, **0** false hits; an unanchored `\d{9}`
fires on a hex digest about four times in five. Week 5 made that mistake, 6.5 made it again in a
query, and it is one constant now. **The never-a-name rule kept a sixth time with its boundary
asserted**: four document-shaped screens may transcribe a name — and the two write receipts came off
that list when the case was first run, because `partiesWritten` is a count and not a who.
