## Sharpened against the plan

**An identifier is never bulk-approved, at any stance**; the plan excluded it only for a viewer who
may not read one. A ת.ז. does not reach the screen until somebody asks for that row, so
`אישור כל מה שלא סומן` would sign a value the signer has not been shown. 90% is no argument: the
number is minimum **OCR word legibility**, which says nothing about whose digits they are. Hence the
column is `איכות הקריאה` and never `ביטחון`, and `null` is flagged rather than passed.
