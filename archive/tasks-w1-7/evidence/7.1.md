## The measurement, and two defects it found

`docs/corpus/lease-amendment.md`, 9 clauses, authored to the published נספח. Corpus 7 → 8.

1. **The title-only lease declaration is refused, on evidence.** `['חוזה שכירות|הסכם שכירות']` alone
   and the live three-requirement declaration verify **the same two specimens and refuse the same
   six**. The corpus cannot separate them, so 6.8's body terms stay: they cost nothing measurable and
   guard `termination_notice`, which has no specimen yet.
2. **`lease_amendment`'s declaration was wrong in both directions**, invisible until a specimen
   existed. `['נספח', 'לחוזה השכירות']` **verified `bank-guarantee.md`** — `נספח` is every annex there
   is, and `לחוזה השכירות` matched the guarantee's `של חוזה השכירות` once whitespace came out — and
   **refused a real נספח**, which is titled indefinitely. Replaced red-first with the annex's own
   title (4 spellings, one requirement) plus `תקופת השכירות`.

**One thing no declaration can fix.** A נספח verifies as a lease and always will: it recites the
document it amends, so its vocabulary is a superset of the lease's, and the guard says *must contain*
with no way to say *must not*. Written into the matrix as one named ordered pair, asserted in both
directions rather than left as a hole; a third specimen in the lease slot still fails it.
