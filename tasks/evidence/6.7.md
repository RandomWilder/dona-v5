## Found by walking, and this is why the slice exists

**A12's address reader has never seen a line break in production, and the first scan it met on
staging read the city as `כפר סבא דירה מספר 3 המשכיר`.** `documentText`
(`src/evidence/internal/verify.ts:47`) joins a page's words with a space and puts a newline only
*between pages*, so the clause `place.ts` and SPEC-evidence.md both rely on — "on a form a line
break is where a field ends" — describes text this system cannot produce. What has always saved it
is punctuation: the spec's own worked example is `כתובת המושכר: רקפת 12, שוהם.`, and the full stop is
what stops the city. Proved three ways: the live processor's own `document.text` reads correctly
(newlines intact), the same text space-joined reads `כפר סבא דירה מספר 3 המשכיר`, and adding one
full stop to the paper fixes it. **The refusal was correct and wrote nothing** — no row, no object,
a candidate list of the building's three flats — which is A12 working exactly as specified while
being wrong about the city. **Not fixed here:** line-aware text is `src/kernel/ocr.ts` and
`src/kernel/pdf.ts`, which is kernel and plan-mode, and not a patch inside the demo slice. Raised
as **6.8**. The paper was reprinted the way the spec's example prints it and all four leases were
re-verified against the live processor before being filed.
