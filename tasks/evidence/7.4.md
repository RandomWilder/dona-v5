rather than a value. Three layers say it: the command (`conflict`), 0029's
`extracted_field_promotion_needs_approval()`, and the ledger drawing no `קדם` on an unsigned row.

**The confirm signs what it promotes rather than being exempted.** A2 and A3 promote without anyone
opening the ledger, and are entitled to — those screens *show* the dates — so `signAndPromote` writes
the approval as read, with `confirmed_by` as approver, leaving alone a row already signed. An
exemption would have covered nearly every promotion this system performs: a rule about nothing.
