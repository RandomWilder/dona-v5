## What was proved

- **One pointer.** `extracted_field.document_type_field_id` is the schema version. No
  `schema_version_id`, no `field_key` column, no `promoted_*`. Two tenants are two rows.
- **Two engines.** The model schema has no `bbox` / `page` / `confidence`. Geometry is the union of
  numbered words from pdfjs or Document AI. A fake reply that includes a bbox is ignored; the stored
  box matches the word, not the invention.
- **A8's open half.** `notice_days` added via `upsertDocumentTypeField` mid-test; re-extract writes
  it; `information_schema` column count on `extracted_field` does not move.
- **Fail closed.** Unconfigured extractor: file stays, zero extracted rows.
- **`-- pii` on `extracted_field.value`**, qualified in guard three (a bare `value` would fire on
  `config_settings`).
- **Work runner started** in `serve.ts`. A runner only claims kinds it has registered, so a tick
  cannot steal another suite's jobs on a shared database.
