## What was proved

- **Staging is capture.** GET/POST `/documents/:id/tenancy` recomputes from `extracted_field` plus
  `getUnit`. No staging table. A6 still recomputes protocols from stored bytes.
- **A verified lease with no tenancy link redirects to confirm.** Unverified stays on the filed page.
  Upload to an existing letting is unchanged (3.3 + 4.3).
- **Role is a POST field.** Missing role is `invalid`. Zero `guarantor_name` rows is success. Two
  `tenant_name` rows are two parties. `createParty` is always an insert; no name match.
- **Cross-check.** Apartment and address folded (strip `דירה`, whitespace). Mismatch: `invalid`,
  transaction rolls back, no party.
- **Dates** still become truth through FieldPromotion (`tenancy.start_date` / `end_date`) and
  `tenancy_event`. Names are not new promotion targets. Party provenance is `PARTY`/`SIGNATORY`
  plus `evidence.confirm_lease`.
- **`terms_profile`** is looked up by name, never defaulted. Missing name is `invalid`.
- **Parking/storage placeholders.** One-row `upsertUnitRow` creates three spaces: `UNIT`,
  `PARKING` `חניה {n}`, `STORAGE` `מחסן {n}`, assigned on the unit. Same standing as מסירה dates
  copied from the lease. Shoham’s 60/40 stay the plan import.
